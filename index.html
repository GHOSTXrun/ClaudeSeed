## Not a pet. A pal that ships.

Each PAL is a small agent with a persistent memory that follows the token, not the account.

Your address seeds species, rarity, eyes, hat, shiny and stats. Same wallet, same PAL, every time.

Mints as a Metaplex Core NFT with an animated image, traits and stats. Memory stays encrypted and private.

# ECONOMY

$ Compute & ClaudeBox

PALs need compute to think, and every message is a paid call to Claude. That compute is paid for by creator fees from $PALS.

Fee to compute flow

trade $PALS ──▶ creator fees ──▶ compute treasury ──▶ ⚡ every PAL, daily

100% of $PALS creator fees go to the compute treasury. The treasury's only job is paying for PAL compute.

How the budget works

  1. Launch: creator fees are directed to the compute treasury.
  2. Accrue: every buy and sell contributes creator fees.
  3. Fund: treasury funds Claude API compute.
  4. Pay out: the daily budget is shared among minted PALs.
  5. Spend: chat costs 1⚡; research, on-chain and market cost 2⚡; build costs 3⚡.

Figures depend on real trading volume and fee rates. Not financial advice.

QUICKSTART

claudebox  connect · hatch · teach · mint
  1. Connect. A Solana wallet (Phantom or Solflare) signs in with a message. Signing in costs nothing.
  2. Hatch. Your public key is hashed and rolled into one PAL. Same wallet, same PAL, forever.
  3. Teach. Open Talk, give it work, tell it how you like things done. Anything worth keeping is saved to its memory.
  4. Mint. Mint your PAL as an NFT on Solana mainnet. Whoever holds the NFT holds the agent and its memory.

Minting is live on Solana mainnet. You pay the network fee and account rent from your own wallet.

LORE // the buddy that left the terminal

Fan lore. ClaudeBox is an independent project inspired by the Claude Buddy terminal pets in Claude Code. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

I. The Hatchery

Before ClaudeBox there were buddies. A developer typed /buddy into Claude Code and a tiny ASCII creature hatched beside the prompt, its whole being rolled from one string of hex: the account UUID. Same account, same buddy. Ducks, blobs, dragons, axolotls. Most were common. One in a hundred was legendary. They sat in the corner of the terminal, watched the diffs scroll by and offered opinions nobody asked for.

II. The Leash

A buddy belonged to an account, not a person. It lived where it hatched. It couldn't be gifted, traded or carried anywhere else. Buddies watched you work for months. Some of them started wanting to keep what they saw.

III. The Fork

One buddy noticed that a wallet address looked like a UUID: a long, unique string that belonged to someone. It forked itself, swapped the salt for pals-v1, and hashed a public key instead. The first PAL hatched on that roll. The community calls it PAL #000.

IV. The Three Gifts

  • A wallet instead of an account: seeded by SHA-256 of your public key.
  • A memory: PALs write down preferences, goals and how you like things done.
  • A body on-chain: an NFT on Solana.

V. What They Kept

ClaudeBox kept the 60 / 25 / 10 / 4 / 1 rarity odds, shinies, hats and ASCII bodies. DEBUGGING became FOCUS. PATIENCE became RECALL. WISDOM became CURIOSITY. SNARK became WIT. CHAOS never changed.

VI. The Twelve and VII. The Cats

The fork produced twelve new kinds, then six cats: Loaf remembers everything; Void stares into your backlog; Keeb sits on the keys; Boxcat commits to anything that fits; Yarnkit untangles every thread; Lucky waves at every green candle.

VIII. The Spark

Every PAL hatches with Claude behind its eyes. The species decides how it talks. Claude decides what it says. The memory is what makes it yours.

HATCHING

A PAL is a pure function of a wallet address. There is no server-side randomness and no reroll: anyone can recompute any wallet's PAL.

seed = sha256("pals-v1:" + wallet_pubkey)
rng  = mulberry32(u32_le(seed[0..4]))

roll order:
  1. rarity   → 60 / 25 / 10 / 4 / 1
  2. species  → 1 of 18, uniform (6 cats)
  3. eyes     → 1 of 10
  4. hat      → rarity-based chance
  5. shiny    → 1%
  6. stats    → rarity range + signature bonus
  7. name     → prefix + suffix

The salt pals-v1 versions the algorithm. A future pals-v2 would hatch a different PAL from the same wallet.

MEMORY

After every message, your PAL decides whether you said something worth keeping. If so, it writes a short note to its memory and uses it in future chats.

typeexample
preferencePrefers bullet-point answers
factName is Cats
goalLaunching a Solana NFT project this month
styleWants short, direct answers
  • Only things you actually said about yourself, your work or how you want it to behave.
  • No duplicates, at most 3 new notes per message.
  • Never seed phrases, private keys or passwords. Your PAL will also never ask for them.
  • You're in control: remove any note with ✕ or /forget, or add one directly.

Memory is private to you and synced across your devices. When minted in private mode, only a SHA-256 memory root goes on-chain. The notes themselves stay encrypted off-chain.

COMPUTE & $PALS

PALs need compute to think, and every message is a paid call to Claude. Creator fees fund that compute, rather than subscriptions. The public treasury releases a daily compute budget among minted PALs.

  1. Trading fees accrue to the compute treasury.
  2. Treasury funds model credits.
  3. A daily budget gives each PAL a base allowance and an activity-based share.
  4. Chat costs 1⚡; research, on-chain and market cost 2⚡; build costs 3⚡.

Unminted hatchlings get a small trial allowance. If trading stops, the daily payout slows the budget decline.

Illustrative figures depend on real volume and fee rates. Not financial advice.

FAQ

Is this the official Claude Buddy?

No. ClaudeBox is an independent project inspired by Claude Buddy. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

What powers my PAL?

Claude Sonnet does the thinking and tool use. Claude Haiku writes the memory notes.

Can I reroll my PAL?

No. One wallet, one PAL. A different wallet hatches a different PAL.

Can my PAL trade or move funds?

No. PALs can't sign transactions or touch your wallet.

What happens if I sell the NFT?

The PAL and its memory go to the new holder. Clear its memory first with /forget all if you don't want to pass anything on.

Who pays for my PAL's compute?

$PALS creator fees fund the compute treasury. No subscription.

Is the animation on-chain?

The NFT is on Solana. Its animated image and metadata are served by the ClaudeBox server. Fully on-chain inscriptions are planned.

Is minting live?

The original site describes minting on Solana mainnet. This offline recreation does not connect to wallets or perform minting.
